## PMV — Portable Music Visualizer

Plays MP3/FLAC/Ogg with real-time MilkDrop visualizations on your handheld.

### Controls
- **ESC / Start** — quit
- Drop song.mp3 (or any audio file) next to the binary, or pass as first argument.

### Presets
Place `.milk` preset files in `presets/` or next to the binary.
